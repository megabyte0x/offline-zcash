# Zcash community prior art

**Research cutoff: 25 October 2026.** External projects were inspected, not run
or audited; licences are as observed, not a dependency audit. Reports below are
not current-app features or new policy. See [current architecture](design.md)
and [accepted repair decisions](components/expiry-reissue.md#proposals-and-owner-decisions-26-october).

A builds/proves/signs, B accepts pending risk, C is the proposed v1.1 co-signer,
D submits raw bytes. Current v0.7.7 uses Common 4 and `ozm-mesh`; co-signers, Tor
and independent chain proofs are unbuilt. Open/unknown payers never get pending acceptance.

## What changes for our decisions

1. **Budget offline expiry separately.** [ZIP 218](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0218)'s 120 blocks preserve ~50 minutes after target height. Zkool2 uses 3,456 for human FROST; ZIP 318 uses 30–60-day migration buckets. None chooses our carriage budget. [#3317](https://github.com/zcash/zcash/issues/3317) discussed offline height/zero expiry in 2018; zero cannot bound recovery.
2. **Separate four clocks:** view freshness, tx validity, C's reservation lifetime and confirmation policy. No fetched source justifies thirty-minute acceptance. That is ~72 NU7 blocks, exceeding H+20; values remain provisional. Expiry does not cancel debt or prove non-inclusion.
3. **Fix expiry at preparation.** [#2380](https://github.com/zcash/librustzcash/issues/2380): “set once at prepare, honored at sign.” C/D must not refresh silently. [#2619](https://github.com/zcash/librustzcash/issues/2619) studies long-lived pre-signing, not a shipped general offline wallet.
4. **Distinguish re-anchor from re-issue.** [ZIP 229](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0229) / [374](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0374): compatible re-anchoring keeps txid/signatures but needs proof repair; expiry/branch changes need new authorization. Preserve auth digest and reservations. [#754](https://github.com/zcash/zcash/issues/754) is replacement precedent, not prevention.
5. **Ten confirmations is demo policy, not finality.** [ZIP 315](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0315) recommends ten for untrusted TXO spendability, three for trusted TXOs. [Kraken](https://support.kraken.com/articles/203325283-cryptocurrency-deposit-processing-times) listed 50 (~62 minutes before NU7). ZIP 218 warns against rescaling without a security model.
6. **Test C's failure model.** Locks must exclude conflicting valid authorizations across concurrency, retries, nonce persistence, crashes, rollback, cloning, re-issue, loss and recovery. An honest, non-rollbackable C is required; unavailability locks funds. Signing, derivation, backups and application-state reviews are separate.
7. **Recheck upgrades.** The [NU8 framework](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu8-decision-framework/54670) and [tracker #1281](https://github.com/zcash/zips/issues/1281) supplied no committed activation height. Use authenticated configuration, not a guessed deadline.

## Adjacent findings to reuse later

- Proof RPC gap is real: [Zebra discussion #9196](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/discussions/9196), [issue #9270](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/issues/9270). Current nodes retain peaks, `zcash_history` 0.5/0.6 has no proof API; reuse our [MMR PoC](../poc/mmr-anchor-poc/README.md), not an assumed deployed endpoint. lightwalletd's inspected `CompactBlock.header` was unpopulated and `GetTreeState` a frontier, not this bundle (code-only finding).
- [Multi-endpoint failover](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/auto-failover-sdk-for-zcash-lightwalletd-multi-endpoint-resilience-for-wallets-apps/52808) / tonic balancing improves availability, not adversarial history comparison.
- Mesh discussion's ~228-byte Meshtastic payload / speculative 450-byte Tachyon tx is not our 9,166-byte Ironwood v6. [ZK-LoRa](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-zk-lora-zero-knowledge-privacy-layer-for-ai-to-ai-mesh-networks/56463) had RF/proof packet prototypes, wallet integration future; [satellite distribution](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/private-money-in-the-sky/57402) was a concept, not bidirectional payment delivery.
- Logos node plumbing / fail-closed routing may transfer later; wallet core combines sign/broadcast with no raw tx/PCZT boundary ([gateway.md](components/gateway.md#logos-phase-facts)). Not a completed offline queue or adversarial chain check.
- [Apple Silicon proving discussion](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/apple-silicon-zero-knowledge-proving-mobile-halo2-and-tachyon-acceleration-on-m-series-and-a-series-chips/56725) supplied generic circuit work; [entry-level Android study](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-wallet-performance-on-entry-level-android/57929) planned results for 30 Nov. Neither establishes Ironwood iPhone timing/RAM/battery. Nozy mobile remained in development; older Zondax shielded hardware signing was v5, not Ironwood v6.
- Konclave reported differing WebAuthn PRF behavior across synced Apple devices / QR flows. Do not assume synced passkeys recover identical FROST-share encryption keys.
- [ZIP PR #420](https://github.com/zcash/zips/pull/420) bearer/key-URI disclosure (closed unmerged) does not establish exclusive authority if the sender retains keys. [SA-SMS](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/proposal-sa-sms-a-frost-powered-gateway-for-shielded-humanitarian-aid/54671) remains a useful adjacent proposal/contact despite funding rejection.

## Searches that returned no qualifying result

No qualifying result in indexed forums/grants, ZIP issues/PRs, blogs and repository
discussions. **Not proof of nonexistence:** private/R&D Discord was not exhaustive;
librustzcash Discussions was unavailable, so issues/PRs were used.

1. Deployed offline Zcash wallet with durable exclusive nullifier locks, crash/backup rollback handling and safe expired-payment replacement matching C.
2. Production restart-safe Zcash-over-BitChat payment queue, or equivalent goTenna/Logos durability contract.
3. Shipping recent-anchors/history-path RPC matching the full bundle; ordinary root/block RPCs are not that endpoint.
4. Shipping lightwallet client cross-checking adversarial histories from independent servers, not merely failover/balancing.
5. Maintained privacy-preserving Zcash public pushTx with established isolation/retry guarantees, or Dandelion as Zcash's deployed default.
6. Committed NU8-or-later activation date/height.
7. Quantitative Zcash basis for thirty-minute offline acceptance, regulator-style amount caps or a universal merchant confirmation threshold.
8. Comparable Ironwood proving benchmarks on the intended iPhones/Android devices.
9. Representative mainnet deep-reorg dataset or typical anchor-age distribution adequate for choosing policy.
10. Documented technical reason for SA-SMS rejection.
11. Verified current Zingo Tor-broadcast semantics or offline **spending** in the collected release material; [offline mode](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zingo-release-announcements/46895/20) was view-only.
